**Draw transmutation circles and duel rival alchemists in a tiny low-poly meadow.**

In Cirquimia the spell *is* the gesture. You are a state alchemist dropped into a procedurally generated meadow that looks like a tabletop diorama, and every attack, wall and heal comes from a symbol you draw with one stroke. Sketch a square and the earth erupts under your rival; draw a circle and a stone wall rises in front of you.

### How to play

1. **Walk:** WASD or the arrows (or click the ground). On a phone, tap the ground or use the stick.
2. **Draw a symbol** anywhere with one stroke. It goes into your hand.
3. **Click or tap a target** to cast it.

| Symbol | Spell |
| --- | --- |
| Circle | Stone wall that blocks strikes |
| Square | Earth: stone pillars erupt under the target |
| Triangle ▲ | Fire: charge it by drawing it over a campfire |
| Triangle ▼ | Water: charge it by drawing it over a lagoon |
| Rhombus ◆ | Heal |
| Pentagram ★ | Five strikes around the target |
| Spiral | Vines that root the target |

Combine them: fire on water makes a steam blast, and a star cast with fire or water burns or floods all five points.

### Features

- A new meadow every match, with campfires, lagoons, ruins and rune circles that boost your power.
- 0 to 3 rivals, free-for-all, on four difficulties. Rivals walk no faster than you; they just draw faster.
- A first match that teaches by playing, and a training mode with no rivals.
- A global daily and all-time ranking with a shareable card.
- Every sound synthesized in code: chalk on slate, the ground breaking open, flame, splash, and a very quiet meadow.
- English and Spanish.
